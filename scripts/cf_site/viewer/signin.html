Sign in with Hugging Face

The Inspect logs of the experiment library are stored in a private Hugging Face dataset. This viewer reads them with your own HF sign-in. Nothing is stored on a server: the access token stays in this browser tab, on this site only, and is forgotten when you close the tab or sign out (at most 8 hours).
